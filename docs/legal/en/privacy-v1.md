## 7. Your rights

Under ZZPL and GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** — know what data we process and obtain a copy.
- **Rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — when conditions of Art. 30 ZZPL are met.
- **Restriction of processing** — in certain cases.
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured format.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
- **Lodge a complaint** with the **Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection** of the Republic of Serbia (www.poverenik.rs).

Send rights requests to [LEGAL REVIEW: email]. We will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures, including:

- encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS);
- encrypted password storage (bcrypt);
- access limited to authorised Studio personnel;
- regular backups and monitoring;
- contractual obligations on IT processors.

In the event of a data breach with risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Commissioner under Art. 52 ZZPL.

## 9. Minors

The app may be used by minors, but only with consent of a parent or legal guardian. The Studio will confirm parental consent in writing at the first visit.

## 10. Cookies and tracking

The mobile app does not use cookies in the classic sense. We use only technical identifiers necessary for the app to function (session token, push token).
