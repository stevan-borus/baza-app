### 3.6. Special categories of personal data

In certain cases we may process data relating to the health condition of the user, where the user voluntarily provides such data through the Application or in communication with the Studio. This data may include information about:

- current and past injuries;
- physical limitations;
- health conditions relevant to performing training;
- pregnancy or post-partum recovery;
- other information the user chooses to share for the purpose of adapting training.

The stated data is processed solely in order to adapt training to the user's individual needs; to protect the user's health and safety during exercise; and to provide adequate support from the instructor. Such data constitutes a special category of personal data under applicable personal data protection regulations and is processed exclusively on the basis of your explicit consent. The user is not obliged to provide the stated data, but in that case the Studio may not be able to fully adapt the training to the user's individual needs. Whenever there is a need to process any of the stated personal data, your explicit consent will always be required.
