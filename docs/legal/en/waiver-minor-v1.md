I agree that the minor for whom I am a parent or legal guardian may use the Studio's services and the Baza mobile app, in accordance with the Terms of Service, the EULA and the Privacy Policy, which I have read and accepted on the minor's behalf.

## 2. Voluntary participation and awareness of risk

The minor participates in pilates training voluntarily. I am aware that pilates as a physical activity carries certain risks, and I confirm that the minor has no medical contraindications, or that I have informed the Studio of any relevant health conditions.

I accept, in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Serbia, **responsibility** for any injury or medical condition the minor may sustain during or as a consequence of training, except where caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct on the part of the Studio.

I confirm that I, as parent/guardian, am familiar with the Studio's rules and that I have explained them to the minor:

- booking and cancellation rules;
- equipment rules;
- conduct rules on the premises.

I undertake, together with the minor, to follow these rules.

## 5. Personal belongings

I agree that **the Studio is not liable for loss or damage to personal belongings** brought to the premises by the minor.

## 6. Processing of the minor's personal data

I consent to the Studio processing the minor's personal data (including date of birth and my own data as parent/guardian) for the purposes of:

- membership records;
- organising and delivering training;
- invoicing;
- communicating with me as parent/guardian about sessions and related information,

in accordance with the Studio's Privacy Policy and the Serbian Law on the Protection of Personal Data.

## 7. Recording of sessions (optional)

I will give or refuse consent for publication of recordings of the minor on social media separately. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

## 8. Written confirmation of consent

I understand that, in addition to this electronic acceptance, **the Studio will request my signature on a written waiver during the minor's first visit**. I agree that the minor may book sessions before signing the written waiver, but that continued use of the service after the first attended session depends on the written signature.

## 9. Acceptance

By accepting this waiver in the Baza app, I confirm that I:

- have read and understood this document;
- accept it in its entirety **on behalf of the minor** as parent or legal guardian;
- agree that acceptance metadata (date, time, IP address, device) is stored as evidence of consent;
- accept that the Studio may verify my statements and request additional documents proving guardianship if required.
