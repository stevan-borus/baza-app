# Consent for processing of health data

**Version:** 1
**Effective date:** 25 May 2026

> Translation note: In case of any discrepancy between the Serbian and English versions, the Serbian version prevails.

## What we collect

Through the in-app questionnaire we collect the following categories of data:

- **Health history**: conditions you select from a predefined list (e.g.
  neck / back pain, disc herniation, scoliosis, joint pain / injuries,
  osteoporosis, high blood pressure, dizziness, recent surgery, pregnancy
  / postpartum). Optional free-text field for other conditions.
- **Medical treatment and medication**: whether you are under treatment or
  taking medication that may affect exercise; if yes, a brief description.
- **Pilates experience**: your prior experience level (mat / reformer /
  clinical Pilates) and approximate duration.
- **Lifestyle and activity**: general daily activity level and weekly
  exercise frequency.
- **Training goals**: selected from a list (e.g. posture, pain reduction,
  flexibility, core strength, rehabilitation, stress reduction, movement
  quality) plus a free-text field.
- **Body awareness**: movements during which you experience discomfort
  (sitting, standing, walking, bending, rotation, balance).
- **Additional information**: free-text for anything else you want your
  instructor to know before training.

## Purpose of processing

The data is used **solely** to allow your instructor to adapt training to
your health condition and goals. It is not used for marketing, profiling, or
sharing with third parties.

## Consent is voluntary

Providing these answers is voluntary. If you choose not to share this
information, you accept that we cannot specifically adapt your training.

You may withdraw consent at any time from your profile. Withdrawing consent
permanently deletes the data you entered, and your instructor will no longer
see it.

## Who has access

Only Studio instructors and administrators, who are bound by a professional
confidentiality obligation, have access to your data.

## Legal basis

Article 17 of the *Personal Data Protection Act* — your explicit consent for
processing of special categories of personal data (health data).
