## 1. Voluntary participation and awareness of risk

I am participating in pilates training at Baza Pilates Studio of my own free will. I understand that pilates is a physical activity that involves certain risks (muscular strain, injury, aggravation of existing medical conditions).

I confirm that I have no medical contraindications for training, or I have informed the Studio and the instructor of any relevant health conditions, and where necessary I have obtained a doctor's opinion on my fitness to participate.

## 2. Personal responsibility

I agree that I am **personally responsible** for any injury or medical condition arising during or as a consequence of my participation in training, except where caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct on the part of the Studio, in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Serbia.

## 3. Studio rules

I confirm that I am familiar with the Studio's rules, including:

- the booking and cancellation rules (cancellation deadlines, consequences of no-shows);
- the equipment rules (pilates apparatus, mats, props);
- the conduct rules on the premises (changing, hygiene, respect for other participants and instructors).

I undertake to follow these rules at all times.

## 4. Personal belongings

I agree that **the Studio is not liable for loss of or damage to personal belongings** brought onto the premises. I understand that I am responsible for safeguarding my valuables and, where possible, using lockers provided by the Studio.

## 5. Processing of personal data

I consent to the Studio processing my personal data solely for the purposes of:

- membership records;
- organising and delivering training;
- invoicing and payment;
- communicating with me via the app, email and phone about sessions and related information,

in accordance with the Studio's Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law.

## 6. Health information

I understand that the Studio may ask for information about injuries or medical conditions in order to adapt training. **Providing this information is voluntary**; if I choose not to share it, I accept that the Studio cannot adapt training to circumstances of which it is not aware.

## 7. Recording of sessions (optional)

I will give or refuse consent for publication of session recordings on social media separately, through the app, and that consent may be withdrawn at any time.

## 8. Acceptance

By accepting this waiver in the Baza app, I confirm that I:

- have read and understood this document;
- accept it in its entirety;
- agree that acceptance metadata (date, time, IP address, device) is stored as evidence of consent.
